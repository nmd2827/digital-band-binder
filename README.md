# Digital Band Binder — Kennedy High School Bands

Phone-first digital binder for the John F. Kennedy High School band program
(Waterbury, CT), directed by Nick Drabik.

- 18 instrument binders plus class binders (Marching, Symphonic, Beginning,
  Jazz, Percussion, Section Leaders)
- Parts, full scores, and part MP3s (with metronome) for the concert book:
  Toboggan, Sleigh Ride, Party Rock Anthem, i will, Smells Like Teen Spirit,
  Big Ballin', and more
- Searchable PDF library, McAllister warm-ups, fingering charts, and the
  Harmony Director (HD-300) tuning guides

Open `index.html` — the site is a single static page (PDF.js renders the
music in-page from `assets/`).

Published from the Muse artifact "Digital Band Binder" (v36, Nov 2026).
Some of the sheet music in here is licensed performance material for the
Kennedy band program.
